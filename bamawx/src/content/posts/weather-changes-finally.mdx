Thanks to an upper level low and a trough, North and Central Alabama are experiencing fog and cool temperatures this morning following three days of much needed rainfall. Rainfall totals over the past three days have been in the 1-3 inch range in portions of the state.  
  
I saw two rainbows yesterday (one just after 7 a.m. and one just after 6 p.m.) but wasn't really in a good position for a photo. My rainbow photos never seem to do justice to what I am actually seeing.
