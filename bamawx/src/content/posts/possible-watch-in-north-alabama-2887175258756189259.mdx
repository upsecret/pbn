  MESOSCALE DISCUSSION 0403  
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK  
0619 PM CDT SUN APR 05 2009  
  
AREAS AFFECTED...MIDDLE TN...NRN AL  
  
CONCERNING...SEVERE POTENTIAL...WATCH POSSIBLE  
  
VALID 052319Z - 060045Z  
  
ISOLATED SEVERE STORMS WILL REMAIN POSSIBLE FOR A FEW MORE HOURS  
WITH MAINLY A HAIL AND WIND GUST THREAT. A BRIEF TORNADO CANNOT BE  
RULED OUT.  
  
STORMS CONTINUE TO FORM ALONG THE COLD FRONT EXTENDING SWWD INTO  
NERN MS.  THIS AREA IS ALSO ON THE TAIL END OF SUBSTANTIAL MID LEVEL  
FORCING ASSOCIATED WITH THE UPPER VORT.  
  
SURFACE ANALYSIS SHOWS A NARROW ZONE OF LOW TO MID 70S F  
TEMPERATURES AHEAD OF THIS LINE OF STORMS. PORTIONS OF NRN AL AND  
SRN TN WERE ONLY IN THE MID 60S F...BUT SOME SLIGHT WARMING MAY  
OCCUR AS THE LINE APPROACHES.  
  
WIND PROFILES ARE QUITE STRONG...AND PERHAPS TOO STRONG FOR  
SIGNIFICANT SEVERE GIVEN ONLY WEAK INSTABILITY IN PLACE.  
HOWEVER...ENOUGH INSTABILITY EXISTS GIVEN THE STRONG FORCING AND  
DEEP LAYER SHEAR FOR SEVERE STORMS CAPABLE OF PRODUCING DAMAGING  
WIND GUSTS...HAIL...AND PERHAPS A BRIEF TORNADO.  
  
..JEWELL.. 04/05/2009
