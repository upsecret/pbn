...FREEZE WARNING IN EFFECT FROM 2 AM TO 7 AM CDT TUESDAY...  
...FREEZE WARNING IN EFFECT FROM 2 AM TO 7 AM CDT WEDNESDAY...  
  
THE NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE IN HUNTSVILLE HAS ISSUED A FREEZE  
WARNING...WHICH IS IN EFFECT FROM 2 AM TO 7 AM CDT TUESDAY. A  
FREEZE WARNING HAS ALSO BEEN ISSUED FROM 2 AM TO 7 AM CDT  
WEDNESDAY.  
  
A VERY COLD AIRMASS WILL IMPACT THE TENNESSEE VALLEY WITH FREEZING  
TEMPERATURES EXPECTED TONIGHT AND TUESDAY NIGHT. TEMPERATURES IN THE  
UPPER 20S TO AROUND 30 DEGREES WILL DEVELOP AFTER MIDNIGHT  
TONIGHT...AND LINGER THROUGH THE EARLY MORNING HOURS. A HARD FREEZE  
WITH TEMPERATURES BELOW 28 DEGREES IS POSSIBLE IN RURAL VALLEYS AND  
THE HIGHER ELEVATIONS.  
  
ON TUESDAY NIGHT...TEMPERATURES WILL AGAIN FALL BELOW FREEZING AFTER  
MIDNIGHT. THE COLDEST TEMPERATURES...IN THE UPPER 20S...WILL OCCUR  
IN SHELTERED VALLEY LOCATIONS...WITH HIGHER ELEVATIONS REMAINING  
AROUND 30 DEGREES.  
  
PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...  
  
A FREEZE WARNING MEANS SUB-FREEZING TEMPERATURES ARE IMMINENT OR  
HIGHLY LIKELY. THESE CONDITIONS WILL KILL CROPS AND OTHER  
SENSITIVE VEGETATION.
