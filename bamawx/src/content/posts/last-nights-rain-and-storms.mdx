Radar estimates that some portions of Franklin County received over 3" of rain. Several other spots received well over one inch. Just about everyone received significant rain.  
  
Numerous [CoCoRaHS reporters](http://www.cocorahs.org/ViewData/ListDailyPrecipReports.aspx) received over one inch so far.  
  
There were numerous reports of hail across the state, some [as large as baseballs near Dora in Walker County](http://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/lsr/#BMX/201010250355/201010250355/0100) and also in [Linden in Marengo County](http://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/lsr/#BMX/201010250045/201010250045/0100). An unconfirmed tornado was reported in Baldwin County last night.  
  
As I write this, the NWS Mobile just indicated a [report of a tornado in the city of Mobile](http://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/lsr/#MOB/201010251020/201010251020/0100) this morning.
