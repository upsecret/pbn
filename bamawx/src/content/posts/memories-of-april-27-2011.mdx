## A generation-defining outbreak

April 27, 2011, produced numerous strong and violent tornadoes across Alabama and the Southeast. For many readers, this blog has long served as a place to share **memories**, **photos**, and lessons learned.

### Forecasting context

The setup featured classic **high-shear, high-instability** parameters across the state. Multiple rounds of storms increased exposure for communities that might have cleared one warning only to face another line hours later.

> If you lived through it, you remember the sound of sirens, TV meteorologists staying on air for hours, and neighbors checking on neighbors.

## Today

Use **multiple warning sources**, know your **safe place**, and drill your plan before the next **moderate** or **high** risk day returns.
