SPECIAL WEATHER STATEMENT  
NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE HUNTSVILLE AL  
941 PM CDT MON APR 6 2009  
  
ALZ009-010-TNZ076-096-097-070900-  
JACKSON-DEKALB-MOORE-LINCOLN-FRANKLIN TN-  
INCLUDING THE CITIES OF...SCOTTSBORO...FORT PAYNE...LYNCHBURG...  
FAYETTEVILLE...WINCHESTER  
941 PM CDT MON APR 6 2009  
  
...MINOR SNOW ACCUMULATIONS POSSIBLE TONIGHT...  
  
AS AN UNSEASONABLY DEEP TROUGH OF LOW PRESSURE PUSHES INTO THE  
REGION...SCATTERED TO NUMEROUS SNOW SHOWERS WILL CONTINUE ACROSS  
SOUTHERN MIDDLE TENNESSEE...AND NORTHEAST ALABAMA TONIGHT.  
  
WARM GROUND TEMPERATURES WILL LIMIT SNOW ACCUMULATIONS. HOWEVER...  
AS THE AIR CONTINUES TO COOL AND SNOW PERSISTS...SOME LIGHT  
ACCUMULATIONS UP TO ONE HALF INCH ARE POSSIBLE ON GRASSY AND  
ELEVATED SURFACES. A DUSTING OF SNOW MAY EVEN ACCUMULATE ON LESS  
TRAVELED SIDE ROADS...MAINLY IN THE HIGHER ELEVATIONS. AREAS IN  
HIGHER ELEVATIONS HAVE THE GREATEST CHANCE OF SEEING ACCUMULATING  
SNOWFALL...AS BOTH AIR AND GROUND TEMPERATURES WILL COOL THE  
FASTEST THERE.  
  
STAY TUNED TO NOAA WEATHER RADIO...OR YOUR FAVORITE MEDIA OUTLET...  
FOR THE LATEST INFORMATION ON THIS LATE SEASON COLD SNAP.
