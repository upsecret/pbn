WSR-74C radar from the old WSO Huntsville, which replaced the WSR-3 radar in 1977.  
  
[![](/images/posts/tour-of-nws-huntsville/76f2e1e0b2895e.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjVZvlwSAYHYgee2qY0tt0faCuglirA8ObEP5rz_0mosAUShWkWBDFE5Yw3naRmVrrOhANRkhZGJKfLpdsV4fqZANW7QgUl6dzJlhilPPcPz-J9diE6CSKhFvU595e4DzewdMsSPg/s1600-h/100_9872.jpg)  
Andy is pointing at a poster of the last F5 tornado in Iowa prior to last year's Parkersburg EF5. I think it occurred on June 13,1976.  
  
[![](/images/posts/tour-of-nws-huntsville/7db1362e83d6ec.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhYox8cYJw0rz8MOQHL6qI_PbMIQzdFdD9BXbbXPo7WiJ0UCHYv738BD5o7-J9wqTngh7UeGfyxZOJLqmvdZc9alHOynYRYf56Eu96Wz_KmP8WyEdePxiSYzTAz8waBe6kB9tC1MA/s1600-h/100_9866.jpg)  
Andy is showing us a velocity image of the EF2 tornado that hit Madison, Alabama on May 6, 2009. This image was provided by the Baron Services radar. Images from this radar were sent via email and Facebook to the NWS to assist in the warning process. Wind Velocity was over 80 mph at an elevation of 1100 feet in this image.
