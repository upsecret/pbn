Here is a short video of the snow in the Huntsville-Decatur area on Thursday January 7, 2010.  
  
  
  
Snowfall amounts across north Alabama ranged from a dusting to 1.3" in Valley Head. Daniel Lamb posted a [county-by-county list on the NSSTC Collaborative Weather Blog](http://nsstc.uah.edu/cwb/?p=2038).  
  
The Decatur _Daily_ covered the snow and cold in an [article linked here](http://www.decaturdaily.com/detail/51045.html). In the article, National Weather Service Meteorologist Chelly Amin said, “Everyone is excited for the snow, but what we are most concerned about are the freezing temperatures for this weekend.”
