Snow accumulations Thursday evening were greatest north of Birmingham and south of Huntsville, especially in northern Jefferson County, western and northern Blount County, and Cullman County. Some people reported up to three inches of snowfall. Visibility in downtown Cullman was low late Thursday afternoon as bands of heavy snow fell across the city.
