1:50 p.m. Columbus Nexrad  
  
A wet and stormy afternoon is beginning across North Alabama. Watches have been issued to the west. [A severe thunderstorm watch is in effect for northern Mississippi and western Tennessee](http://www.spc.noaa.gov/products/watch/ww0172.html). Storms are lining up. [A watch may soon be issued for westeren Alabama](http://www.spc.noaa.gov/products/md/md0525.html). I will be watching things closely on radar. There is a chance I will get out and do some storm spotting, but it is unlikely I will drive far from home.  
  
A tornado warning is in effect for Washington County in southwestern Alabama.
