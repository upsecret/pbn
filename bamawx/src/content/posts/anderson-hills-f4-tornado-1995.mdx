## May 18, 1995

The **Anderson Hills** tornado remains one of the notable violent tornadoes tied to the Huntsville metro area’s storm history. Understanding these events matters for both awareness and for honoring communities affected.

### Why it still gets discussed

- Strong, long-track supercell environments across the Tennessee Valley are well documented.
- Damage surveys and radar archives help us teach **storm-relative** thinking and safe sheltering today.

> Historical posts on BamaWX are for education and remembrance — not a replacement for real-time warnings.
