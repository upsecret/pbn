If you live in North Alabama and have in interest in the weather, or have children interested in science, consider attending the secon annual [**Rocket City Weather Fest**](http://www.rocketcityweatherfest.com/flyer.pdf) this Saturday, September 17, 2011 at UA Huntsville's Shelby Hall from 11 a.m. until 4 p.m.  
  
The event is free and open to the public. Find out all of the details at [**www.rocketcityweatherfest.com**](http://www.rocketcityweatherfest.com/).
