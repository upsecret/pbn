So far there have been 14 tornadoes confirmed from Thursday 2/24/11. Kentucky had 4, Arkansas had 3, Tennessee had 3, Alabama had 2, Louisiana had 1, and Mississippi had 1. Five of these tornadoes were rated EF2. There were 258 high wind reports, [according to the SPC](http://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/110224_rpts.html).  
  
There was no severe weather in my part of Huntsville, Alabama, but I did capture a bit of video as the storm rolled through.

Local Date: 02/24/2011  
Local Time: 11:10 PM CST  
County: CULLMAN  
State: AL  
Source: NWS STORM SURVEY  
  
AN EF-0 TORNADO WITH MAXIMUM WINDS OF 75 MPH AND A PATH WIDTH OF 20 YARDS TRACKED ABOUT ONE QUARTER MILE ON A FARM JUST EAST OF BATTLEGROUND. THE TORNADO FIRST SNAPPED AND TWISTED FOUR TALL HARDWOOD TREES BEFORE ADVANCING NORTHEAST AND DESTROYING A LARGE BUT UNOCCUPIED CHICKEN BARN. METAL AND WOOD DEBRIS WAS SHREDDED AND TWISTED AMONGST NEARBY TREES AND DAMAGED THE ADJACENT HOME AND A MOBILE HOME.
